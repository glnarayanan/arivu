## Design Context

### Users

Arivu serves technical readers, researchers, operators, and small teams who self-host a private knowledge workspace. They use it to capture web pages and notes, retrieve prior research, review material before it disappears into an archive, and administer a small instance. Everyday flows should stay approachable for smart users who do not need infrastructure details; administrative flows may use precise technical language when it helps operators act safely.

### Brand Personality

Opinionated, durable, and tactile. The interface should feel calm and dependable during focused reading and review, direct and reassuring during errors or long-running processing, and confident about local ownership. Copy is factual, compact, and human rather than glossy or promotional.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Arivu's Brightlight-derived warm editorial direction: white and pale-sand
canvases, near-black neutral text, a restrained coral accent, serif editorial
headings, sans-serif UI and prose, fine dashed rules, compact radii, pill
controls, and sparse soft shadows. Arivu is light-only; keep
`color-scheme: light` even when the operating system prefers dark appearance.
The reference theme changes look and feel only, so preserve routes, navigation,
menu layout and options, information hierarchy, and functionality. Do not ship
or depend on Brightlight's Astro/Tailwind implementation, fonts, images,
scripts, or other assets. Avoid gradients, glass panels, dark surfaces, heavy
borders, oversized shadows, decorative motion, excessive saturation, and
dependency-heavy UI patterns. Preserve accessible contrast, visible focus,
reduced motion, touch-friendly controls, and narrow-screen layouts.

### Design Principles

- Keep the shipped frontend dependency-free and native-browser-first.
- Use strong hierarchy, visible state, and direct labels instead of decorative complexity.
- Use dashed neutral rules for structure, compact radii for fields and surfaces,
  pills for actions and states, and shadows only where layering is functional.
- Make controls reliable for keyboard, touch, slow networks, long text, and narrow viewports.
- Reinforce the warm archive identity through shared semantic tokens and layout rhythm, not one-off styling.
- Prefer compact, useful product copy over marketing language or unexplained technical jargon.
