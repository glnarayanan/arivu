## Design Context

### Users

Arivu serves self-hosting readers, researchers, operators, and small teams who use a private bookmark archive to save pages, search prior material, review archived content, and administer their own instance.

### Brand Personality

Opinionated, durable, tactile. The UI should feel like low-dependency software with a physical archive quality, not glossy SaaS.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the warm-paper brutalist direction: ink-heavy borders, condensed display type, serif reading text, mono UI labels, and a restrained red, teal, gold, and ink palette. Avoid generic SaaS cards, glass effects, purple gradients, and dependency-heavy UI conventions.

### Design Principles

- Keep the embedded frontend dependency-free and native-browser-first.
- Make keyboard, touch, loading, empty, error, and long-content states feel intentional.
- Use direct, compact product copy.
- Reinforce the archive aesthetic through shared tokens and spacing rhythm.
- Treat the browser UI as flagship polish for a self-hosted app.
